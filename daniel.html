A Personal Message
A Message from Daniel Di Giorgio

Dear Resident,

I am proud to be running for City Councillor in York South-Weston. I have been a strong local advocate, standing up for residents and delivering real results. We need a councillor who puts the community first. Residents are our most valuable resource, and with reliable leadership, we can achieve remarkable progress together.

Toronto faces serious challenges and City Hall needs common sense. Crime is rising, traffic is worsening, public transit is failing, and property taxes are out of control. I will bring practical solutions, responsible spending and leadership that puts residents first.

It's time for change. Together, we can make our City stronger, safer and more affordable.

About Me

A neighbour. A community advocate.

I've spent a decade taking care of this neighbourhood — knocking on doors, showing up at community events, and working alongside the residents who make York South-Weston what it is. Now I'd like to do it as your councillor.

I'm a graduate of the University of Toronto in Engineering, with a diverse background in project management, consulting, construction, and finance. That experience has given me a practical, results-driven approach to problem-solving — one I intend to bring to every decision at City Hall.

I've also served two terms as a locally elected trustee, advocating for the families and students of York South-Weston. That role taught me how to navigate institutions, push for accountability, and deliver results for the people I represent.

I'm a lifelong resident of York South-Weston — and I'm motivated to see this area, and the City of Toronto, reverse its decline and reclaim its potential.
