# Bill C-19

## An Act to amend the Financial Administration Act (reporting dates)

### Summary

This enactment makes technical amendments to the timing and format of certain departmental reports tabled in Parliament.

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as An Act to amend the Financial Administration Act (reporting dates).

2. The date by which departmental results reports must be tabled is changed from within the first 90 days of the fiscal year to within the first 120 days.

3. Where a report is required to be tabled in both Houses, tabling in one House while the other is not sitting satisfies the requirement, provided the report is tabled in the second House within the first three sitting days thereafter.

4. Departmental plans and results reports may be published in a consolidated electronic format prescribed by the Treasury Board.

5. This Act comes into force on a day to be fixed by order of the Governor in Council.
