# Bill C-274

## An Act respecting the recognition and revitalization of Indigenous languages

### Preamble

Whereas Indigenous languages are fundamental to the identities, cultures, spirituality, and self-determination of Indigenous peoples;

Whereas many Indigenous languages in Canada are at risk of being lost;

Whereas the revitalization of these languages is a shared responsibility;

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the Indigenous Languages Recognition Act.

2. The Government of Canada recognizes that the rights of Indigenous peoples include rights related to Indigenous languages.

3. An Office of the Commissioner of Indigenous Languages is established to promote Indigenous languages, support revitalization efforts led by Indigenous communities, and undertake research and public education.

4. The Minister may enter into agreements with Indigenous governments and organizations to provide for the reclamation, maintenance, and strengthening of Indigenous languages.

5. The Commissioner shall submit an annual report to Parliament respecting the use and vitality of Indigenous languages in Canada.
