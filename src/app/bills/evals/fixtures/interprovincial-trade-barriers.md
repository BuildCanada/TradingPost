# Bill C-101

## An Act to reduce barriers to internal trade and labour mobility

### Summary

This enactment removes federal barriers to trade between provinces and recognizes provincial occupational credentials across Canada.

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the Internal Free Trade and Labour Mobility Act.

2. **Mutual recognition of goods.** A good that may lawfully be sold in one province is deemed to meet federal requirements for sale in every other province, absent a demonstrable health or safety justification.

3. **Credential portability.** A worker certified to practise a regulated occupation in one province is entitled to practise that occupation in any other province without re-examination, subject only to registration.

4. **Federal procurement.** Federal procurement rules shall not favour suppliers on the basis of province of origin.

5. **Burden of justification.** Any federal measure that restricts internal trade must be published together with the specific objective it serves and evidence that no less restrictive means is available.

6. The Minister shall report annually on remaining internal trade barriers and the estimated cost they impose on the Canadian economy.
