# Bill C-401

## An Act to establish National Fiddle Day

### Preamble

Whereas fiddle music has been played across Canada for generations and forms part of the cultural heritage of many communities, including Acadian, Métis, Celtic, and Ukrainian-Canadian traditions;

Whereas the fiddle is recognized as an instrument that brings communities together through dance, celebration, and the passing down of folk traditions;

Whereas it is desirable to recognize and celebrate this shared musical heritage;

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the National Fiddle Day Act.

2. Throughout Canada, in each and every year, the third Saturday in May is to be known as "National Fiddle Day".

3. For greater certainty, National Fiddle Day is not a legal holiday or a non-juridical day.

4. The Minister of Canadian Heritage may support activities and events that recognize the contribution of fiddle music to Canadian cultural life.
