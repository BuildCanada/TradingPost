# Bill S-88

## An Act respecting the National Tartan of Canada

### Preamble

Whereas tartans have long been used to represent communities, families, and shared heritage;

Whereas the Maple Leaf Tartan has been worn at national celebrations and reflects the changing colours of the maple leaf through the seasons;

Whereas it is fitting to formally recognize a national tartan as a symbol of Canada;

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the National Tartan Act.

2. The tartan known as the "Maple Leaf Tartan", the specification of which is set out in the schedule, is recognized as the national tartan of Canada.

3. The national tartan may be displayed and worn on occasions of national significance and at ceremonies honouring Canadian heritage.

4. Nothing in this Act affects any existing provincial or community tartan.
