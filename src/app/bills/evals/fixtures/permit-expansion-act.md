# Bill C-338

## An Act respecting federal oversight of commercial construction projects

### Summary

This enactment creates a new federal licensing and reporting regime applicable to medium and large commercial construction projects.

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the Commercial Construction Oversight Act.

2. **New federal permit.** No person shall commence a commercial construction project with a floor area exceeding 500 square metres without first obtaining a Federal Construction Authorization, in addition to any existing municipal or provincial permits.

3. **Advisory panels.** The Minister shall establish regional advisory panels which must be consulted, and which may hold public hearings, before any Authorization is issued.

4. **Quarterly reporting.** The holder of an Authorization shall file quarterly progress reports, workforce composition disclosures, and materials-sourcing declarations with the Department throughout construction.

5. **Fees.** The Minister may prescribe application, review, hearing, and annual maintenance fees payable by proponents.

6. A proponent who proceeds without an Authorization, or who fails to file a required report, is liable to a penalty for each day of non-compliance.
