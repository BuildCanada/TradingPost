# Bill C-212

## An Act to establish a National Resource and Energy Corridor

### Summary

This enactment creates a pre-approved national corridor for energy, mineral, and transportation infrastructure to accelerate the movement of Canadian resources and products to domestic and export markets.

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the National Resource and Energy Corridor Act.

2. The Minister shall designate a network of rights-of-way (the "Corridor") suitable for pipelines, electrical transmission, rail, and road infrastructure connecting resource-producing regions to tidewater and to interprovincial markets.

3. **Streamlined approvals.** A project situated within the Corridor that meets the published environmental and safety standards is deemed to have satisfied the federal siting review, and no further federal route hearing is required.

4. **Single-window review.** The Minister shall establish a single office responsible for coordinating all federal permits for Corridor projects, with a binding service standard of 24 months from complete application to final decision.

5. **Investment certainty.** Proponents who receive Corridor designation are entitled to fiscal terms that remain fixed for the life of the approval, to encourage long-term private capital investment.

6. The Minister shall report annually to Parliament on the volume of exports enabled and private capital invested through the Corridor.
