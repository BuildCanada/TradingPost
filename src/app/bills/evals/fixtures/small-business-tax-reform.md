# Bill C-155

## An Act to amend the Income Tax Act (small business growth incentives)

### Summary

This enactment reforms the tax treatment of small and medium-sized enterprises to reward reinvestment, hiring, and productivity-enhancing capital purchases.

### Enactment

1. This Act may be cited as the Small Business Growth Incentives Act.

2. The small business deduction limit is increased, and the associated clawback tied to passive investment income is repealed, so that growing firms are no longer penalized for accumulating capital.

3. **Immediate expensing.** A qualifying corporation may fully deduct, in the year of acquisition, the cost of new machinery, equipment, and productivity software, in place of multi-year depreciation.

4. **Hiring credit.** A refundable credit is provided against the incremental payroll cost of net new full-time positions created in a taxation year.

5. **Reduced compliance.** Corporations below a prescribed revenue threshold may file a simplified single-page return, replacing the existing multi-schedule filing.

6. The Minister of Finance shall review the measures after five years and report to Parliament on their effect on business investment and job creation.
